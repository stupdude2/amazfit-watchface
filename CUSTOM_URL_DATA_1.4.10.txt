Big Time 1.4.10 Custom URL Data

- Adds one configurable public HTTP/HTTPS plain-text data source.
- Optional 12-character label; blank label uses established no-label sizing.
- Refresh intervals: 5, 15, 30, 60, 120, or 360 minutes.
- Last successful value is cached on phone and watch.
- Custom URL appears in top/bottom side and center data selectors.
